## Profil (laut Website, Originaltext auf Englisch)

Helmut Ahr has been a member of the Executive Board of the international management consultancy Horváth since 2012 and has served as CEO since April 2022. In this role he oversees the Controlling & Finance and Technology & AI consulting divisions. He also leads the firm's Marketing, Communications & Sales service unit.

He graduated from Friedrich-Alexander University Erlangen-Nuremberg with a degree in Business Administration. He began his career in a series of management roles in the CFO organisation of Landesbank Baden-Württemberg. He joined Horváth in 2000, later led its Financial Industries sector, and was subsequently appointed to the Executive Board.
