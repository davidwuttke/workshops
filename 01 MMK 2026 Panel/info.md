Ihr Panel: „Can Industrial AI Turn Germany's Weaknesses into Strengths?“
What if the biggest obstacle to Industrial AI is not the technology, but the way companies are organized? As Germany faces rising costs, skills shortages and growing global competition, can AI help overcome these structural disadvantages and restore industrial competitiveness? How can industry fundamentally reinvent people, processes and products – rather than simply making existing workflows more efficient?
