## Profil (laut Website, Originaltext auf Englisch)

Sarah Engel shapes the future of industrial innovation as Head of Artificial Intelligence at TRUMPF. Her focus is on scaling AI so that it delivers real, measurable value, with trust and responsibility at its core.

She previously held leadership positions at IBM and Capgemini, where she guided organisations from their initial AI strategy through to enterprise-wide rollout. Her academic background in cognitive science and neuroinformatics (Universities of Tübingen and Ulm) informs how she aligns technology with human and organisational needs.
